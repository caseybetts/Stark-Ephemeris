# Development roadmap

This roadmap captures the current high-level planning sequence for project development. It is design context, not a detailed task list or a commitment that every item is fully specified. The [Pending Changes](PENDING_CHANGES.md) document remains the on-deck menu for selectable implementation work.

The layers are intended to build on one another: establish the core experience and operating loop, deepen the simulation, add business growth systems, then tune and refine the complete experience. Their ordering is a planning guide; it does not settle the open rules or implementation choices documented in the gameplay and technical briefs.

## Layer 1 — Core experience (mostly finished)

- GUI and visualizations
- Global Order Deck
- Scheduling behavior

## Layer 2 — Operational simulation depth

- Ground contact dynamics
- Basic Revenue Generation
- Spacecraft subsystems

## Layer 3 — Company growth and feature expansion

- Company finances
- Spacecraft building and launching
- Outages, Errors and Anamolies

The [reviewed Layer 3 plan](LAYER_3_PLAN.md) records BIZ-02's implemented purchase and antenna flow, then sequences a future-launch upgrade and one bounded outage/response scenario. It keeps the remaining gameplay decisions visible.

## Layer 4 — Tuning and refinement

- Fine-tuning playable attributes and revenue generation
- GUI refinement
